# Privacy Policy for Transformice Wiki Tracker

Transformice Wiki Tracker is built as a privacy-friendly browser extension.

- **Data Collection:** The extension does not collect, store, or share any personal information, telemetry, or browsing history.
- **Local Storage:** All user data (such as your checked/owned items) is stored exclusively locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your browser.
- **Contact:** If you have any questions, you can reach out via GitHub.
